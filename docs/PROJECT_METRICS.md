## Claims held out of public promotion

- **End-to-end scientific publication:** blocked pending an authorized, provenance-cleared non-test observation, exact ArcGIS credential/item and privacy verification, and readback through the private service, public views, and dashboard. See [publication record](PHASE12_ARCGIS_PUBLICATION.md) and [release gates](../project-control/RELEASE_GATES.md).
- **iOS 1.0 available to testers:** the 1.0.0 (17) workflow was cancelled before signing and upload; the checkpoint's read-only App Store Connect query found no Build 17. The earlier 0.1.0 (13) TestFlight result belongs to an older SHA and does not transfer to this candidate.
- **Current Android release or instrumentation pass:** Android is deferred until after the iOS submission/publication milestone. A previous emulator outage ran no instrumentation tests; it does not prove a product defect or a passing current device run.
- **Test and historic samples as environmental reach:** controlled test data and the historical inventory with unresolved provenance are excluded from public monitoring claims. Do not use their raw records, site labels, or counts in portfolio graphics.
- **Completed semester hours, impact, users, or institutional endorsement:** no such headline is supported by this candidate. The weekly plan is a schedule, not an attestation.
- **Clean review or security clearance:** the five UltraReview finding texts were unavailable at the pre-merge checkpoint, so none has a verified disposition. A separate [Copilot review posted after PR #42 merged](https://github.com/UnbrokenMango21/watershed-water-quality-dashboard/pull/42#pullrequestreview-5334777741) lists three open comments (one high and two medium) about account enforcement parity, Android CI scope, and a stale reviewer-profile lookup. They are review reports requiring assessment, not validated defects or the missing UltraReview five.
