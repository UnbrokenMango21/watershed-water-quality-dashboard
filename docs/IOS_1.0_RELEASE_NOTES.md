## TestFlight: what to test

This release candidate updates the PA Watershed Watch identity and refines the field collection and review flow. Sign in with the provisioned test account, choose a test site, enter a method and supported measurements, and review the visit before submission. Check that saved drafts remain after relaunch, sync status is clear, and a requested correction creates a new revision while preserving the submitted one.

Use test accounts and sites only. Do not submit a real environmental observation unless the program coordinator authorizes it. Report problems through TestFlight feedback with the device model, iOS version, and steps to reproduce. Do not include passwords, private observations, reviewer details, or internal site labels.

Photo and audio capture are outside this release candidate. TestFlight verification does not establish scientific approval or public publication.
