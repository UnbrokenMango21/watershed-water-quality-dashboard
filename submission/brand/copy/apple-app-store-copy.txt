=== Description (plain text) ===
PA Watershed Watch is a field-data tool for authorized Pennsylvania watershed monitoring teams. Accounts are provisioned by your monitoring program. There is no public sign-up.

IN THE FIELD
• Sign in with the account your program provisioned for you.
• Select the monitoring site you are sampling.
• Record the sampling visit, including GPS location accuracy.
• Enter water-quality measurements with their scientific units.
• Save drafts on the device and return to them before you submit.

REVIEW
• Submit completed observations to your program's private review workflow.
• Automated validation checks help reviewers find values that need a closer look. They support human scientific review and do not replace it.
• Corrections are saved as new revisions. Earlier submitted revisions are preserved.

PUBLICATION
Observations that pass human review may later be published by the program to a public ArcGIS dashboard. PA Watershed Watch does not determine whether water is safe for drinking, swimming, fishing, or any other use.

NOT IN THIS VERSION
Photo, audio, and other media capture are not available yet.

Questions about access? Contact your program coordinator or the support contact listed on this page.

=== TestFlight: What to Test (0.1.0 (13)) ===
Thanks for testing build 0.1.0 (13). Please use a physical iPhone and field-like conditions where you can.

1. Sign in with the provisioned test account your program coordinator gave you.
2. Select a test monitoring site and start a sampling visit. Check that GPS accuracy is shown and recorded.
3. Enter measurements. Confirm each unit is correct and clearly labeled.
4. Save a draft, force-quit the app, reopen it, and confirm the draft is intact.
5. Turn on Airplane Mode, save a draft, reconnect, then submit. Note whether the sync status is clear at every step.
6. Submit an observation and confirm it shows the expected review status.
7. Submit a correction and confirm the earlier revision is still listed.

Send anything confusing, slow, or incorrect through TestFlight feedback with a screenshot. Use test sites only unless your coordinator says otherwise.

Not in this build: photo, audio, and media capture.
